# Labelling protocol: ground truth v3 (pre-registration R2)

The computed ground truth (`../ground_truth.yaml`) is generated by our own code, so it needs a check that does not come from the code. These labels are that check. They are not used to tune anything.

## Who

- **Two team members** label independently.
- Ideally neither of them wrote the repair code under evaluation.
- They do not discuss scenarios until both sheets are finished.

## Blind conditions

While labelling, **do not**:
- run VoyageSync or any `eval/` script;
- open `../ground_truth.yaml`, `eval/results*`, `eval/golden/` or any repaired plan;
- read `eval/scenarios/labels.yaml`. Those older labels reason from one specific repair policy, which is exactly what v3 avoids.

Use only `briefs.md`, which shows each trip **before** the disruption, the dependencies between its tasks, and the alternatives the planner may choose.

## The question (one row per scenario × task)

> Considering **every** acceptable repair the planner could make with the options listed, must this task be re-planned in **all** of them?

- Answer `yes` or `no` in `must_change`.
- Optionally give a `confidence` (1 = unsure, 3 = certain) and a `note`.
- If at least one acceptable repair could leave the task untouched, the answer is `no`, even if most repairs would change it.

## Rules that define an acceptable repair

**What the planner can't change:**
- A delayed flight keeps its new times: the delay is reality, and that flight cannot be swapped.
- A storm stays forecast.
- A cancelled flight or an overbooked hotel *must* be replaced, by an option other than the one cancelled.
- Tasks that are not re-planned keep exactly their current plan.

**What must hold afterwards:**
- Every dependency listed under "Dependencies" holds.
- The trip dates and the number of travellers stay as booked.
- Every day except possibly day 1 keeps at least one activity. Day 1 may be empty only if the arrival drop-off + 60 min, rounded up to the next quarter hour, is after midnight.

**What doesn't block a repair:**
- The budget is soft: going over budget does not make a repair unacceptable.

**When a task counts as re-planned:**
- A task is re-planned when one of the dependencies it relies on would otherwise be broken.
- The budget is re-planned whenever any task's cost changes. The budget is recomputed from its line items.

**Disruptions with no acceptable repair:** for example, an arrival after the hotel's check-in cutoff. Answer `no` for every task, and write `no acceptable repair` in the note of each row.

## Steps

1. Copy `annotator_template.csv` to `annotator_A.csv` and `annotator_B.csv`. Each annotator fills in their own copy.
2. Check the agreement:
   `backend\.venv\Scripts\python.exe eval\labels_v3.py agree eval\scenarios_v3\labels\annotator_A.csv eval\scenarios_v3\labels\annotator_B.csv`
   This reports Cohen's κ and every disagreement.
3. Adjudicate: for each disagreement, the two annotators agree on one answer and record the reason in `note`. Save the result as `adjudicated.csv`.
4. Compare with the computed ground truth:
   `backend\.venv\Scripts\python.exe eval\labels_v3.py compare eval\scenarios_v3\labels\adjudicated.csv`
5. Report κ, the exact-agreement count and every mismatch.
   - A mismatch is **reported, never tuned away**.
   - If a mismatch shows a bug in the code (an option or rule that is wrong), fix the code, regenerate the ground truth, and record the change under `deviations` in `eval/preregistration.yaml`.
   - Otherwise the computed ground truth stands, and the mismatch is discussed in the paper.

## Scope

- 48 scenarios: every disruption level (16) on three trips. These are `santorini_solo_luxury` (international, solo, 5 nights), `goa_couple_domestic` (domestic, couple, 3 nights) and `tokyo_family_week` (international, group of 4, 7 nights).
- Weather tasks are not asked, because they are facts.
- A delayed flight is not asked about in its own scenario, because it is reality.
